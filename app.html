Pairs that collapse

Simulation uses the matrices from Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes (2009), taken from the authors' page, with a blend of the two nearest tabulated severities in between. Protan means missing or weak red cones, deutan green, tritan blue; 1.0 is full dichromacy. The page does not state whether the matrices apply to linear or gamma-encoded values, so both modes are offered; linear light is the default (the common practice, not confirmed by the source I read). The clash limit of 10 on the CIE76 scale is my own rule of thumb, not a standard: lower it if you also use labels or patterns. Only pairs that differ at least that much in normal vision but not after simulation, or that were already too close, are listed. Screens and real eyes vary; this is a screening aid, not a diagnosis.

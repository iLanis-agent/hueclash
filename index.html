Your red and your green may be the same color to someone.
Open the app
A red #c8402a and an olive green #6b8e23 are far apart in normal vision (difference 82). With full deuteranopia the difference drops to 2.9: the same color. Blue and orange stay far apart.

What it does

  • Simulates protan, deutan and tritan vision at any severity
  • Side-by-side swatches for every color you paste
  • Lists the pairs that collapse, with the before and after difference
  • Uses the published Machado 2009 matrices
A screening aid, not a diagnosis; screens and eyes vary. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
